5. One Month on NammaMetro

Remember that NammaMetro fails to report ridership numbers on some days, so the monthly ridership totals calculated from the data are only approximate. However, we can still identify trends and patterns by analysing data movement on a granular level.

The plot below reveals strong weekly cycles — each weekend dip is followed by a sharp recovery, demonstrating the metro's vital role in weekday commuting. The high variability in aggregate data suggests we should segment our analysis to reveal patterns currently masked by day-to-day fluctuations.

Distribution of daily ridership by month. The box shows the IQR; the line inside is the median; dots are outliers.

A striking inverse relationship exists between commuter and casual users — reinforcing the dual nature of the metro system. The total ridership line shows how the two segments combine to create a more stable overall pattern despite their individual volatility.

📘 Notes — Data gaps are shown with dashed lines, maintaining data integrity while still allowing for trend analysis. February 2025 has a narrower box than December 2024, suggesting more consistent day-to-day ridership despite lower overall numbers.
