3. One Week on NammaMetro

From late October to early February, the metro carried about 8 lakh riders a day. The rhythm was that of a city that works hard and plays hard.

Ridership has a strong weekly cycle. Weekdays look like weekdays; weekends look like weekends. The interesting story is which channels dominate on which days.

👆🏼 Surprising find: Smart Card and NCMC usage peaks during the week; Tokens and all forms of QR peak on weekends. Click on the payment methods to cross verify. What does this suggest about the ridership?

From Monday to Thursday, Smart Card and NCMC usage — typically used by regular metro riders commuting to work — averages ~450,000 and ~12,000 respectively. Tokens and QR payments, typically used by non-regular metro riders, reach peak usage on Sundays. Weekend and holiday demand is driven by casual users — road users who switch to public transport on the weekends.
