4. Three Traffic Bands, Two Kinds of Patrons, One Metro

Ridership starts to dip on Fridays (perhaps due to liberal work-from-office policies in the IT industry or commuters preferring to use their personal vehicles.) For another segment of workers, Saturdays are as much a working day as the rest of the week.

On Sundays, Smart Cards+NCMC usage sees a ~60% drop from average Weekday levels. Tokens+QR increases by ~15%. This confirms the presence of two distinct types of users: Commuters and Casual Users.

💡 Three Traffic Bands — Weekday (Mon–Thu), Weekend Lite (Fri–Sat), Weekend (Sun). The visualisation validates the hypothesis about Bangalore's distinct traffic bands and rider categories.

👆🏼 Surprising find: Smart Card and NCMC usage peaks during the week; Tokens and QR peaks on weekends.

💡 Two Kinds of Patrons — NammaMetro thus exhibits a dual role: a commuter necessity on weekdays, and a weekend leisure service.
